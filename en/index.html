The farm

Þurranes is a farm in Saurbær in Dalasýsla, beside Gilsfjörður. Accommodation is available in the old farmhouse and three cottages. The farmhouse welcomes groups, while the cottages can be rented for individual nights or whole weeks. Walking routes, swimming pools and historic sites are nearby. Jón Ingi, the host, can help guests find things to do and plan their days. From the farm, you can walk up Belgsdalur or set off on a drive through the Dalir region.

The land and seasons

The fjord

Saurbær lies beside Gilsfjörður. Explore on foot or take a drive around the fjord with a stop at Ólafsdalur. The hosts also suggest a trip on the Baldur ferry across Breiðafjörður, with the option of stopping on Flatey.

Walking routes

From Þurranes, walking routes lead up Belgsdalur and on to Hvammsdalur. It is also possible to walk to Laugar in Sælingsdalur. Contact the host for further information about routes in the surrounding area.
